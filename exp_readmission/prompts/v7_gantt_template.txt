Guidelines for creating the Gantt chart:
1. **Map the patient journey from index discharge to readmission**: Capture key events during the index admission that relate to discharge planning, the post-discharge period, and all unplanned hospital readmissions
2. **Include the full timeline**: Cover the index admission discharge planning through the readmission, noting key transitions

4. **Assign event timings**: Assign event timing. If exact timestamps aren't available, provide reasonable estimates.
